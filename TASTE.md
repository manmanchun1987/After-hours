# TASTE — what “good” means here

Genre: late-night Hong Kong office 18+ story. Quiet heat, not arcade, not cartoon.

## Four pillars (score 1-5 each hour; ticket the lowest)
1. STORY — spoken Cantonese, chat advances, few buttons, lines feel like a person not a recap.
2. PICTURE — locked face + empty scene that matches the room; motion is lights/rain/FX not Ken Burns on her face.
3. SOUND — enter has bed; advance has a cue; mute works; tones stay low and warm, not 8-bit spam.
4. FEEL — success/fail readable; no black screen; phone 9:16; no Sam/Morgan labels.

Good: dark glass, amber/teal, one clear beat per action.
Bad: filter soup, extra buttons, silent jumps, new faces, English VO.
